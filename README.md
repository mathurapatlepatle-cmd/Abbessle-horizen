Yahan "Abyssal Horizon" ka complete Game Design Document (GDD) hai — jo Sunkenland ke core mechanics par based hai, lekin ultra-realistic 4K graphics, deep ocean survival, crystal clear water physics, aur "4D" sensory/dynamic world mechanics ke saath design kiya gaya hai.
1. Game Concept & "4D" Experience
 * Game Title: Abyssal Horizon
 * Genre: Open-World Underwater & Ocean Survival, Base-Building, Multiplayer Co-op / PvPvE
 * Engine: Unreal Engine 5.4+ (Lumen global illumination, Nanite micro-polygon geometry, aur custom Fluid Flux water simulation).
 * 4K & Crystal Clear Water Tech:
   * Caustics & God Rays: 4K volumetric light shafts jo suraj ki kirno ko paani ke andar tak realistic refraction ke saath dikhate hain.
   * Zero-Murk Visibility: Paani bilkul crystal clear hoga jahan aap 200 meter door tak sunken skyscrapers, coral reefs, aur deep-sea creatures ko bina kisi dhundh (fog) ke saaf dekh sakenge.
 * 4D Mechanics (Time, Dynamic Environment & Sensory Feedback):
   * Dynamic Tide & Weather Cycles: Samundar ka level badhta-ghat-ta hai, storms aate hain, aur underwater currents aapki swimming speed aur boat control par asar dalte hain.
   * Haptic & Spatial 3D Audio: DualSense / haptic feedback ke saath paani ka pressure, distant whale calls, aur metal creaking mehsoos hota hai.
2. Core Gameplay Loop (Sunkenland Inspired + Upgraded)
| Feature | Gameplay Mechanics |
|---|---|
| Deep Ocean Diving | Drowned megacities, sunken nuclear submarines, research labs, aur deep abyssal trenches (1000m+ depth) ko explore karein. Depth ke hisaab se Pressure Suit aur Oxygen Tanks upgrade karne honge. |
| Modular Base Building | Samundar ki satah par floating fortress banayein, kisi abandoned aircraft carrier par kabza karein, ya phir paani ke andar Glass Dome Underwater Base banayein jahan se bahar ka crystal clear view dikhe. |
| Survival & Ecosystem | Hunger, thirst, body temperature, aur Nitrogen/Decompression sickness (agar aap bahut tezi se gehrai se upar aate hain). Marine life mein friendly dolphins se lekar aggressive Megalodons aur giant squids tak shamil hain. |
| Vehicles & Traversal | Raft se shuru karke Jet Skis, Speedboats, Gyrocopters, aur deep-sea Mini-Submarines (Seamoth style) craft karein. |
| 200 IQ Enemy AI & Raids | Pirate clans aur mutated deep-sea raiders sirf seedha hamla nahi karte—woh aapke base ke weak points (jaise power generators ya oxygen pipes) ko target karte hain, flank karte hain, aur underwater torpedoes use karte hain. |
3. Multiplayer Modes (Co-op & PvPvE)
 * 4-Player Co-op Campaign: Dosto ke saath milkar ek mega-base banayein, ek banda submarine chalaye, doosra turret sambhale, aur baaki dive karke loot ikattha karein.
 * Dedicated Servers (Up to 32 Players):
   * Trading Atolls (Safe Zones): Neutral floating markets jahan players bina kisi currency ke item-for-item barter trade kar sakte hain.
   * Territory Wars: Oceanic oil rigs aur sunken vaults par kabza karne ke liye clan battles.
4. Progression & Tech Tree (5 Tiers)
 * Tier 1 (Castaway): Wooden raft, spear, basic diving mask, rainwater collector.
 * Tier 2 (Scavenger): Scrap metal base, harpoon gun, oxygen rebreather, sailboat.
 * Tier 3 (Engineer): Solar/wind power grids, underwater glass tunnels, motorboat, assault rifles.
 * Tier 4 (Deep Explorer): Titanium pressure suit, sonar scanner, mini-submarine, automated base turrets.
 * Tier 5 (Abyssal Master): Nuclear reactor, plasma cutter, gyrocopter, electromagnetic defense shield.
5. Graphics & Optimization Settings (4K / 60+ FPS Target)
 * DLSS 3.7 / FSR 3.1 Frame Generation: 4K resolution par smooth 60–120 FPS maintain karne ke liye.
 * Ray-Traced Reflections & Refractions: Paani ki satah aur glass domes par real-time reflection.
